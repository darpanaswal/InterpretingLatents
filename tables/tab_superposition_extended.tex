\subsection{Superposition Probing (Experiment~2): Extended Analysis}
\label{app:superposition_extended}

Three complementary views of the probing signal beyond per-timestep point estimates. Table~\ref{tab:candidate_parallelism} reports cumulative top-$k$ raw probabilities (T1, T3, $\Delta = $ T3 $-$ T1) to quantify how much mass each model spreads across competing candidates. Table~\ref{tab:convergence_trajectories} contrasts $k{=}0$ and $k{=}4$ to summarize whether each model converges (rising $P(\text{correct})$ with falling entropy) or fails to. Table~\ref{tab:per_instance_statistics} breaks the same patterns down per instance, isolating the fraction of problems on which each behavior actually occurs.
